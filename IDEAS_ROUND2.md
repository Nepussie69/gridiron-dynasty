## Coordinator / head coach (20)
1. ★ **Practice week**: split the week's practice between install (new concepts), sharpen (execution) and rest (health). Over-practicing raises injuries; resting too much costs execution.
2. ★ **Keys to the game**: before kickoff pick 2 keys ("take away their WR1", "win the turnover battle", "run for 150"). Graded after the game; hitting them builds staff and player trust.
3. ★ **Personnel packages**: choose base groupings (11/12/21 personnel on offense; base, nickel, dime on defense). They change who plays and match up against the opponent's groupings.
4. ★ **Special teams moments**: onside kicks, fake punts and FGs, return strategy, kick placement as game-day decisions (with tendencies, so fakes stop working if overused).
5. ★ **Injury decisions**: a starter is questionable, so play him (aggravation risk) or rest him. The medical staff's read quality depends on the staff you hired.
6. **Weather and venue**: rain, snow, wind and domes shift passing and kicking. Plan for them in the week; the coordinators flag it.
7. **Challenge flags**: on a close call, challenge it or not (lose a timeout if wrong). Replay odds shown by your staff.
8. **Trick plays**: a small set of limited-use trick plays that work best when rare (flea flicker, double pass, fake FG). Using one makes the next one easier to read.
9. **Halftime speech**: a motivational choice (calm, fiery, accountability) that moves second-half composure, depending on team culture. Separate from the tactical adjustments.
10. ★ **Locker room management**: captains, a star complaining about touches, discipline calls (fine or bench a player). Trust and culture change, and so does performance.
11. **Press conferences**: weekly presser answers (deflect, own it, call out a player) move media, owner patience and morale.
12. **Player development plans**: set a focus for each young player (footwork, route running, pass rush moves). Position coach specialties make some plans stronger.
13. **Signature plays and playbook growth**: installing concepts over seasons unlocks signature plays that become better than the base concept, but opponents study them.
14. **Film study tells**: spending film hours can reveal a specific tell ("they blitz from this front 70% of the time"), a sharper version of the opponent read for one bucket.
15. **Primetime and playoff stakes**: primetime games and playoff weeks carry bigger reputation swings and an extra prep option (all-in plan, fatigue cost next week).
16. **Ask the GM**: as HC, request a position or a specific player from the GM before the deadline or the draft. The GM's trust in you decides whether it happens.
17. **Player trust in your calls**: players trust the play-caller more after wins and good calls; trust nudges execution on key downs, and repeated bad calls cost it.
18. **Bye-week plan**: on the bye, choose self-scout (break your own tendencies), rest (heal), or extra install.
19. **Mentoring your assistants**: as HC, develop your coordinators. Good mentoring grows your coaching tree; they also leave for other jobs.
20. **Referee crews**: crews call more holding or more pass interference. Adjust aggression to the crew (scouted in the week).

## General manager (15)
1. ★ **Trade deadline day**: a deadline event with a flurry of AI offers. Buyer or seller depending on the standings, and contenders overpay for rentals.
2. ★ **Free agency frenzy**: a 3-day bidding window with competing offers, player decision timelines and "they took less to play for a winner".
3. ★ **Draft day trades**: trade-up and trade-down offers while you're on the clock, with time pressure.
4. ★ **Tags and options**: franchise and transition tags, fifth-year options, rookie extensions as yearly decisions with real cap consequences.
5. **Scouting travel budget**: send scouts to all-star games and pro days. Coverage decides how much you know about each prospect.
6. **Incentives and contract structure**: incentive clauses, escalators, void years, option bonuses in negotiations; agents value them differently.
7. **Holdouts and trade requests**: unhappy stars hold out or ask out, so negotiate, trade or wait. Character traits drive it.
8. **Hiring the head coach**: as GM, interview HC candidates with philosophies, and fire one mid-season if needed (owner patience).
9. **Analytics department**: hire analytics staff to sharpen trade value, win-probability and contract models (better staff, better numbers).
10. **Fan sentiment and owner budget**: fan approval moves owner patience and free-agent appeal; facility upgrades cost budget and help recruiting.
11. **UDFA scramble**: right after the draft, a short bidding window for undrafted rookies (signing bonuses, roster promises).
12. **3-year cap planner**: a multi-year cap sheet showing restructures, extensions and dead money before you commit.
13. **Comp pick planning**: show how letting players walk or signing free agents moves next year's compensatory picks.
14. **AI trade market**: AI clubs shop disgruntled or expensive players mid-season; a scouting report on each trade target.
15. **Contract-year motivation**: players in a contract year play a bit better; extend them early (cheaper, less motivation) or wait.
