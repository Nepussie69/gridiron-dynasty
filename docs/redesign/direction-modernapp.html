System notes

  • Team colour, restrained. Raw primary and secondary are used only in large fills: the 3px top band, the crest and the faint top-bar wash. All foreground uses --team-accent, picked per theme to clear 3:1. For CLE that is #FF6A33 in dark and #C43200 in light; the brown primary never sits on dark as text.
  • Ratings. Six tiers on one ramp. Red only means Poor (≤51). The league middle is neutral grey, so a 67 no longer looks like a failure. Staff chips add "−26 vs avg" so 45 and 57 read as different.
  • Destructive actions. They live at the bottom of the ⋯ menu, are red as text only, and always open a consequence sheet. At Advisor access they become "Recommend…".
  • Top bar. Four KPIs each have a tone and a word ("Tight", "Hot seat"). The access level moves into the screen it governs. The sim mode moves into the Advance split menu.
  • Phone. No sidebar. A bottom tab bar (Home, Team, Game, Club, More) plus a swipeable KPI strip. Every top-bar fact stays reachable.
