Rendering

  • Canvas 2D with its own pinhole camera (look-at, yaw, FOV) and near-plane clipping. Field markings are projected polygons; numbers, end-zone wordmark, crowd and LED boards are textures mapped in subdivided perspective cells.
  • Players are code-drawn sprites (helmet with stripe and facemask, jersey and number, pads, arms, legs) posed by state (stance, run, block, throw, catch, carry, dive, fall). Each figure's run cycle is driven by the distance it has covered. Figures are depth-sorted every frame.
  • The phone uses the same renderer with a narrower, taller camera that frames the strong side.
